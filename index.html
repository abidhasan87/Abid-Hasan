About

Research-driven engineering.

I am an Electrical and Electronic Engineering graduate from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET), Bangladesh, with research interests centered on physiological signal processing, machine learning, wearable sensing, and embedded AI.

My research focuses on developing reliable and resource-efficient machine learning methods for physiological signals and wearable health monitoring systems. I am particularly interested in translating machine learning research into practical systems that can operate on resource-constrained edge devices.

Through my research experience, I have worked with physiological signals including PPG, accelerometry, and EDA, as well as deep learning models for healthcare and wearable applications.
